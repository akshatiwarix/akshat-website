Enrichment Waterfall

GitHub

Finds a work email by trying providers in cost order and stopping at the first address that survives verification. Every attempt is recorded: what it returned, why it was kept or thrown out, and what it cost.

  • Deterministic

Signal Scout

GitHub

Persona Mapper

GitHub

A company and an offer go in; a buying committee comes out — titles that actually exist at that headcount, in that industry's vocabulary, with an authority ladder tied to deal size. The same offer restructures the committee completely at eight people and at eighteen thousand.

Why Now

GitHub

Turns dated company evidence into a "why now" hypothesis where every inferential step is licensed by a declared rule — or the chain visibly breaks at the step that failed. Built because a well-cited non-sequitur is worse than no output at all.

  • Synthetic corpus
