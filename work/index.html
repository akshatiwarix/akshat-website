GTM engineering

People ask what a GTM engineer actually does, so here's the honest version: I build the machinery a startup uses to find and win customers. Sales and marketing decide who we should talk to and what we should say. I build the systems that make that happen at scale, and keep them running.

I do this full time at Ollive.ai, an early-stage startup building AI liability insurance. Early stage means the go-to-market motion doesn't exist until someone builds it. That's my job.

Day to day

What I build

Outbound and lead generation

Finding the right companies and people, enriching the data, and getting outreach in front of them. This is the pipeline's front end, and most of my week lives here.

CRM, ops, and analytics

The plumbing behind the pipeline: keeping the CRM clean, wiring integrations, and making sure the numbers we look at are numbers we can trust.

AI agents and internal tools

Wherever a GTM task is repetitive, I try to make software do it. I build AI-powered workflows and internal tools so the team spends its time on conversations, not busywork.

Proof of work

The tool stack

The GTM engineer tool stack changes fast, but right now mine is: Clay and FullEnrich for enrichment, Apollo for prospecting and sequencing, Airtable and HubSpot for keeping the pipeline honest, Granola for meeting notes, and n8n, Zapier, and the Claude and OpenAI APIs for automation and agents.
